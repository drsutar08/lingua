Lingua ("we", "the app") is published by [YOUR COMPANY NAME]. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

## What we store, and where

**On your device only.** Lingua has no account server. When you sign up, your name, email address and a salted, hashed version of your password are stored on your device, together with your learning progress (courses, XP, streak, vocabulary, review schedule and settings). We cannot see this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell or share personal information.

## Microphone and speech recognition

Speaking exercises and conversation practice use your microphone **only while you tap the mic**. Your speech is turned into text by your device's speech service:

- **iPhone and iPad:** Apple Speech Recognition. Apple may process audio on its servers. See Apple's privacy policy.
- **Android:** the Google speech service on your device. Google may process audio on its servers. See Google's privacy policy.
- **Web browser:** the browser's speech service (for example Google in Chrome, Microsoft in Edge).

Lingua receives only the resulting text, uses it to score your pronunciation, and does not save audio.

Daily reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us.
